What it does

Everything happening
on your network.

PCYBOX Orbis pulls back the curtain on your machine's network activity — no technical knowledge required.

Force Graph

Nodes stabilize into a live graph of all active connections. Drag, lock, and explore. Your machine sits at the center.

World Map

Every IP is geolocated and drawn on a live globe. Animated arcs show traffic flowing to datacenters, CDNs, and clouds worldwide.

60-min Timeline

A rolling history of your traffic, stored locally in SQLite. Scrub back in time to see what happened and when.

Anomaly Detection

Automatically flags suspicious patterns — port scans, beaconing, potential exfiltration. Know when something is off.

Process Attribution

Not just IPs — know exactly which app on your machine is generating the traffic. Top 5 processes per connection.

Privacy Score

A real-time score of how much your outgoing traffic is going to trackers, ad networks, or unknown destinations.

Simple by design

Up in 60 seconds.

Install, launch, and your network comes alive. No configuration, no cloud account, no data leaving your machine.

01 — INSTALL

Run the installer

Download the Windows installer (101 MB). Accept the UAC prompt — admin rights are needed for network capture. Npcap installs automatically if missing.

02 — LAUNCH

Open PCYBOX Orbis

The app starts its capture engine in the background. Within seconds the force graph populates with your live connections.

03 — EXPLORE

Map your traffic

Switch between the graph and the world map. Hover nodes for details, check the timeline, review your privacy score.
