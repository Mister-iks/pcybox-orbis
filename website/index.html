01 Features

Everything happening
on your network.

Orbis pulls back the curtain on your machine's network activity - no technical knowledge required.

01

Force Graph

Nodes stabilize into a live graph of all active connections. Drag, lock, and explore your machine at the center.

02

World Map

Every IP geolocated and drawn on a live globe. Animated arcs trace traffic to datacenters and clouds worldwide.

03

60-min Timeline

A rolling history stored locally in SQLite. Scrub back in time to see exactly what happened and when.

04

Anomaly Detection

Automatically flags suspicious patterns port scans, beaconing, potential data exfiltration. Know when something is off.

05

Process Attribution

Not just IPs know exactly which app on your machine is generating traffic. Top 5 processes per connection.

06

Privacy Score

A real-time score of how much outgoing traffic is going to trackers, ad networks, or unknown destinations.

02 How it works

Up in 60 seconds.

Install, launch, and your network comes alive. No configuration, no cloud account, no data leaving your machine.

01
Install

Run the installer

Download the Windows installer (~101 MB). Accept the UAC prompt admin rights needed. Npcap installs automatically.

02
Launch

Open PCYBOX Orbis

The capture engine starts in the background. Within seconds the force graph populates with your live connections.

03
Explore

Map your traffic

Switch between the graph and world map. Hover nodes for details, check the timeline, review your privacy score.
